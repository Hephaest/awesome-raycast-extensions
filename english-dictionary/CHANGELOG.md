# English Dictionary Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search a word in Wiktionary, Collins or Merriam-Webster, or type it straight after the command in root search, and switch dictionaries with ⌘P.
- Open the entry for the text selected in any app with Define Selected Word.
- See every meaning grouped by part of speech, with its definition and examples, a picture when the dictionary has one, and its pronunciation and usage labels underneath.
- Hear each recording the dictionary has, and copy a definition with its source.
- Open the word in Oxford Learner's, Cambridge, Longman, Vocabulary.com, Urban Dictionary or the OED from the Open in Other Dictionary… menu.
- Find your recent words as you type, remove one with ⌃X, or clear them all with ⌃⇧X.
